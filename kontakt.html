Let's talk.

Simulation data from automotive design for crashworthiness has been at the focus of my work for the past four years, and I am particularly fascinated by the potential to rethink established simulation workflows through data-driven and AI-based methods. Through exchanges with leading research institutions and participation in scientific conferences, alongside hands-on method development on real industrial simulation data, I have experienced both the remarkable progress in this field and the challenges that remain unresolved. For me, the most exciting of these is whether, and under which conditions, generative AI can learn the complex interaction of nonlinear effects in crash simulation well enough to generalize beyond its training data.

Would you like to get to know me or talk about data, simulation and AI in engineering? Get in touch via LinkedIn – I look forward to hearing from you.
